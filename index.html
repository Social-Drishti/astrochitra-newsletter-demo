Simple Wisdom

Tips & Information for July

Practical guidance to help you move through the month with balance, clarity and intention.

Health

  • Begin the day with warm water and lemon.
  • Practice Anulom Vilom for 10 minutes.
  • Favour sattvic, freshly cooked meals.

Wealth

  • Review SIPs and avoid impulse spending.
  • Donate 2-3% of income to a good cause.
  • Keep financial documents organised.

Love

  • Listen more than you speak this week.
  • Plan a small ritual together on Fridays.
  • Express gratitude daily to loved ones.

Spirituality

  • Wake up during Brahma Muhurta.
  • Read one verse from the Gita daily.
  • Light a diya every evening at home.
